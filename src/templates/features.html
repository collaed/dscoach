Coaching Relationship Management

Everything you need to manage, track, and develop your coachees — in one place.

Your Dashboard

  • At-a-glance tiles for every coachee: pending tasks, completion rate, average grade, streak, strikes, check-in status
  • Filters: needs grading, no check-in today, has strikes
  • Quick actions from the tile: send a note, give an acknowledgement — no extra clicks
  • Bulk grading — grade all pending submissions on one page

Task System

  • Categories (mental, physical, emotional, admin) and difficulty levels (easy, medium, hard)
  • Assign to one or many coachees at once
  • Task library — save, search, and reuse templates. See usage history per coachee
  • Recurring tasks — auto-assign every N days, with optional variance for unpredictability
  • Reserve tasks — auto-assigned if nothing was sent by unveil time
  • Unveil & freeze times per coachee, in their timezone
  • Task dependencies — Task B unlocks only after Task A is done
  • Partial completion — coachees can mark tasks as partially done
  • Photo attachments on submissions (auto-resized)
  • Grading (A–F) with comments, visible to the coachee
  • Auto-strikes on missed tasks

Communication

  • Bidirectional notes between coach and coachee
  • Scheduled messages — write now, deliver at a specific time
  • Voice notes — record and send audio from the browser
  • Read receipts — see when a coachee read your note
  • Pinned notes — keep important messages at the top
  • Telegram notifications — grades and acknowledgements auto-sent

Check-ins & Tracking

  • Morning and evening check-ins with structured prompts
  • Tracking logs: food, hydration, alcohol, exercise, emotional regulation
  • Immutable and timestamped — no edits after submission

AI-Powered Insights

  • Psychological profile per coachee, generated from all their data
  • Paste historical exchanges (WhatsApp, emails) to enrich the profile
  • Standalone text analyzer — paste any conversation for instant analysis
  • Powered by Mistral AI

Analytics

  • Weekly summary: tasks done/missed, avg grade, check-ins, streak, strikes
  • Compliance heatmap — 90-day calendar, green/yellow/red per day
  • Category breakdown — performance by task type with completion rates
  • Streak tracking — consecutive days with all tasks completed on time

Coachee Autonomy

  • Goal setting — coachees propose goals, you approve or modify
  • Private journaling with optional sharing to coach
  • Progress photos — periodic uploads for visual tracking
  • Self-reflection after each task (mood rating + free text)
  • Data export (JSON) for full transparency

Safety & Trust

  • Safe word & pause mechanism per coachee
  • Contract management with version history
  • Audit log — every login with IP and user agent
  • Password management — coaches change their own, reset coachees'

Personalization

  • Avatars per coachee (20 options)
  • Custom accent color per coachee
  • Coach branding: logo, background, card, and accent colors
  • Timezone-aware — times shown in both coach and coachee local time
